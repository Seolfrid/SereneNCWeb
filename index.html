    About

    Sound that holds
    space without filling it.

    SereneNC is ambient lo-fi built around one idea: the golden hour, indoors. The amber of a lamp after dusk — the quiet that settles when the day finally exhales.

    Warm textures, slow drones, rain against glass. Made for evenings when you need background music that disappears into the room — studying, reading, winding down.

    Free to stream everywhere. If it becomes part of your evenings, consider supporting on Bandcamp or via PayPal.
